Instructions
In a mixing bowl, blend together the oil, brown sugar, and white sugar. Add the baking soda, vanilla, egg, and yolk. Add the flour blend and salt, and mix until a dough is formed. Stir in the chocolate chips and chopped chocolate.
Using 1/2 Tbsp measuring spoon, scoop out the dough onto a parchment lined baking sheet. The cookies spread a lot while they bake so I don’t recommend using a larger scoop…unless you want huge cookies!
350°f for 10 min. Cookies will continue to cook once they are out of the oven so don’t bake for longer than 10 minutes.
