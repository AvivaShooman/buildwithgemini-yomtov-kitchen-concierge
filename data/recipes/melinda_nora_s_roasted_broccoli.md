Ingredients
Raw Broccoli Florets
Olive Oil
Garlic Salt or Onion Soup Mix
Cook Mode
Prevent your screen from going dark
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°f
Mix the broccoli with the olive oil and spices
Spread evenly on a sheet pan and bake for 30 minutes, until the edges are crispy
