# Cabbage and Pomegranate Salad

**Author / Source:** Ruhama Shitrit (RuhamasFood)
**URL:** https://ruhamasfood.com/recipes/cabbage-and-pomegranate-salad

## Content & Preparation

### Instructions
- 1.In a large salad bowl combine the cabbage, cucumbers, chives, basil, and pomegranate seeds.
- 2.In a small bowl whisk together the salt, pepper, lemon juice, white vinegar, olive oil, and minced garlic until emulsified.
- 3.Pour the seasoning over the salad and massage gently with your hands until everything is evenly coated.
- 4.Finish with the garnish of pomegranate seeds before serving.

### Ingredients
- For the salad
- •1 small green cabbage, cut to half and then to thin slices
- •3 Persian cucumbers, finely chopped
- •Handful of chives, finely chopped
- •Handful of fresh basil, roughly chopped
- •1 cup of pomegranate seeds
- For the seasoning
- •1 teaspoon of salt
- •1 teaspoon of ground black pepper
- •Juice from 1 lemon
- •1 tablespoon of white vinegar
- •4 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil
- •1 minced garlic clove
- For the garnish
- •1 tablespoon of fresh pomegranate seeds

### Instructions
- 1.In a large salad bowl combine the cabbage, cucumbers, chives, basil, and pomegranate seeds.
- 2.In a small bowl whisk together the salt, pepper, lemon juice, white vinegar, olive oil, and minced garlic until emulsified.
- 3.Pour the seasoning over the salad and massage gently with your hands until everything is evenly coated.
- 4.Finish with the garnish of pomegranate seeds before serving.
