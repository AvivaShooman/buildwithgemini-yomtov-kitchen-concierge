# Dairy-Free Gluten-Free Chocolate Cake

**Author / Source:** Ruhama Shitrit (RuhamasFood)
**URL:** https://ruhamasfood.com/recipes/dairy-free-gluten-free-chocolate-cake

## Content & Preparation

### Instructions
- 1.Preheat the oven to 400F / 200C.
- 2.In a mixing bowl combine the eggs, coconut milk, maple syrup, and vanilla. Whisk together.
- 3.Add the cacao powder, almond flour, and baking powder and whisk until smooth.
- 4.Grease a round oven-safe pan and pour in the batter, spreading it evenly.
- 5.Decorate the top with walnuts or pecans.
- 6.Bake for 25-30 minutes, until set.

### Ingredients
- Cake ingredients
- •3 eggs
- •1 can of unsweetened coconut milk
- •1/3 cup of maple syrup
- •1 teaspoon of vanilla paste
- •1/2 cup of unsweetened cacao powder
- •3 cups of almond flour
- •1 teaspoon of baking powder
- Topping
- •10-12 raw walnuts or pecans for decorating

### Instructions
- 1.Preheat the oven to 400F / 200C.
- 2.In a mixing bowl combine the eggs, coconut milk, maple syrup, and vanilla. Whisk together.
- 3.Add the cacao powder, almond flour, and baking powder and whisk until smooth.
- 4.Grease a round oven-safe pan and pour in the batter, spreading it evenly.
- 5.Decorate the top with walnuts or pecans.
- 6.Bake for 25-30 minutes, until set.
