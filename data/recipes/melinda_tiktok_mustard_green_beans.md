Ingredients
Green Beans
Olive Oil
Dijon Mustard
Soy Sauce
Garlic Salt
Cook Mode
Prevent your screen from going dark
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°f
Mix the green beans with the mustard, soy sauce, olive oil, and garlic salt
Spread evenly on a sheet pan and bake for 30 minutes, until the edges are crispy
