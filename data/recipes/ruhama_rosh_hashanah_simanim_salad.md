# Rosh Hashanah Simanim Salad

**Author / Source:** Ruhama Shitrit (RuhamasFood)
**URL:** https://ruhamasfood.com/recipes/rosh-hashanah-simanim-salad

## Content & Preparation

### Instructions
- 1.Add all the salad ingredients to a salad bowl.
- 2.Pour the dressing on top and mix well.
- 3.Garnish with pomegranate seeds and chopped parsley.
- 4.Store it in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.

### Ingredients
- •1 package (250 g) of Israeli pearl couscous, cooked and cooled to room temperature
- •2 green apples, finely chopped
- •5 Persian cucumbers, finely chopped
- •4 dates, pitted and chopped
- •1 jalapeno pepper, finely chopped (optional)
- •1 small red onion, finely chopped
- •1 bunch of finely chopped mint leaves
- •1 bunch of finely chopped parsley
- •1 cup of pomegranate seeds
- Dressing ingredients
- •1/4 cup of extra virgin olive oil
- •Juice from 2 small lemons
- •2 tablespoons of pomegranate molasses
- •1 teaspoon of honey
- •1 tablespoon of Dijon mustard
- •2 minced garlic cloves
- •1 teaspoon of salt
- •1/2 teaspoon of black pepper
- •1 teaspoon of sumac
- Topping
- •Handful of pomegranate seeds and chopped parsley

### Instructions
- 1.Add all the salad ingredients to a salad bowl.
- 2.Pour the dressing on top and mix well.
- 3.Garnish with pomegranate seeds and chopped parsley.
- 4.Store it in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.
